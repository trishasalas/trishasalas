Three tiers of specialization — not by layer position, but by what they respond to. Universal heads (blue) bind all compound terms across all domains and cluster in layers 9–15. Domain detectors (red) respond to specific domain clusters and are scattered from layer 5 to layer 34. Domain-unique heads (green) fire for only one domain. Tiers and layer positions are independent — specialization doesn't predict location.

Despite this rich architecture, ablating the universal heads changes mean binding by less than 1%. Toggle "After Ablation" to see compensator heads that absorb the loss.
